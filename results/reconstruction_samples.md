## Hand inspection (2026-09-11)

Every sample was read against its raw tweets, in two rounds.

**Round 1 found six reconstruction defects.** All are fixed, and each has a unit test in
`tests/test_dataprep.py`:

| Defect | Seen in | Fix | Effect on the full data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split parts out of order when posted in the same second | S04 | Order parts by time, then reply-chain depth, then the `1 ^JL` / `2/2` marker | Misordered merged replies: 108 → 9 of 3,604 |
| Context showed only part 2 of a split brand reply | S04, S09, S15, S19 | Brand context turns show the whole merged reply | Fragment-like brand turns: 56 of 6,218 left |
| Replies to brand announcements counted as follow-ups | S08, S21, S22 | A follow-up must answer a brand *reply* | About 900 exchanges reclassified as first contacts |
| 2017 message answering a 2014 tweet: stale context, and a post-split exchange landed in train | S26 | Drop context turns older than 7 days; post-split exchanges of earlier threads go to `excluded` | 117 exchanges excluded; 163 contexts cut |
| DM request counted as substantive ("…so we can have you try a few steps") | S16 | Guidance must sit outside the DM-request sentence | Substantive replies that ask for a DM: 488 → 226 |
| Sign-off glued to the text (`details?^EZ`) left in clean text | (the integrity test) | A `^` tag doesn't need a space before it | Leftover markers: 0.57% → below 0.5% (test passes) |

**Round 2 (current build): all 36 reconstructed correctly.** S04 was re-checked after the
ordering fix. In every sample:
- the customer message is merged correctly
- the brand reply is complete, in order, and has its sign-off and split markers stripped
- follow-up context reads as a conversation
- the split and eval flags are consistent

**Weaknesses that remain.** These are in the labels, not the reconstruction:
- **`reply_type = substantive` is a keyword label.** 4 of the 10 substantive samples are
  clarifying questions or status updates that happen to say "try", "update", "steps" or
  "sign in" (S17, S24, S25, S27). The retrieval corpus is therefore perhaps ~60% true
  guidance, so Phase 7 must re-rank or filter it rather than trust the label.
- **Customer escalation cues are crude.** "ridiculous amount of space" counts as anger. They
  are fine as weak input features, never as labels.
- **9 merged replies (0.25%) still read out of marker order.** Either the brand posted part 2 a
  second before part 1, or re-posted a cut-off tweet. Time order is kept for those.
